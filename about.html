TURNKEY EXECUTION PROCESS

From Topographic Survey to Public Launch

Phase 01

Site Feasibility & Survey

Topography inspection, soil bearing capacity evaluation, guest circulation mapping, and physical anchor point assessment.

Phase 02

3D Design & Load Analysis

Structural 3D CAD modeling, dynamic live-load calculations, wind-resistance engineering, and client blueprint sign-off.

Phase 03

In-House Fabrication

Precision cutting, heavy-duty welding, anti-corrosive hot-dip galvanizing, and factory pre-assembly quality inspections.

Phase 04

Field Erection & Rigging

On-site structural anchoring, column erection, high-altitude cable tensioning, and safety lifeline assembly by certified riggers.

Phase 05

Proof-Load Testing

Calibrated weight testing under simulated multi-passenger loads, emergency descent drills, and structural safety audits.

Phase 06

Staff Training & Handover

Detailed operational SOP manuals, harness inspection protocols, staff emergency response training, and final sign-off.
