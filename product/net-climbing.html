What is Net Climbing?

Net Climbing is an adventure activity that uses a tensioned climbing net for participants to climb upward, across or through a structured course. It can be designed as a standalone climbing activity or integrated into an adventure tower, low rope course or multi-activity adventure setup.

Starline Adventures manufactures customized Net Climbing systems according to the required dimensions, height, user group, site conditions and project requirements.

Net Climbing Design & Engineering

Proper design and structural calculations are essential to ensure net climbing installations handle continuous multi-climber dynamic loads and environmental stresses. Main engineering considerations include:

  • Overall climbing height
  • Net width and span length
  • Net configuration (vertical, inclined, or multi-faceted)
  • Available site area and footprint
  • Support tower structure and mounting attachments
  • Structural loading and perimeter rope tensioning forces
  • Simultaneous participant capacity
  • Target user age group and grip reach
  • Secure anchor points and structural tie-backs
  • Rope and hardware configuration
  • Foundation requirements and ground anchoring
  • Indoor or outdoor installation requirements
  • Site terrain, slope, and elevation
  • Weather exposure and sunlight conditions

Net Climbing Manufacturing Process

Our systematic manufacturing and delivery workflow follows structured engineering milestones:

  1. Requirement & Site Assessment: Reviewing venue footprint, desired climbing height, expected traffic, and participant demographics.
  2. Layout Planning: Designing net inclination, lane divisions, attachment interfaces, and transition platforms.
  3. Engineering & Structural Design: Calculating frame wind resistance, live load dynamic stresses, and anchor tension parameters.
  4. Structural Framework Fabrication: Precision cutting, jig welding, and protective anti-corrosive coating of heavy mild steel supports.
  5. Net/Rope System Preparation: Measuring, cutting, and weaving high-strength UV-resistant netting with reinforced borders.
  6. Hardware & Connection Assembly: Pre-assembling perimeter wire ropes, turnbuckles, thimbles, and rated connection clamps.
  7. Factory Inspection/Testing: Inspecting weld integrity, netting tensile joints, and hardware fitments prior to shipment.
  8. Transportation & Site Installation: Transporting components to the site followed by foundation setting, steel erection, and net tensioning.
  9. Final Inspection & Commissioning: Verifying cable tensions, conducting operational checks, operator guidance, and handover.
