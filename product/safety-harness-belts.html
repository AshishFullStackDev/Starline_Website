Safety Harness & Belts Overview

Commercial adventure parks, ziplines, climbing walls, and high rope courses rely on ergonomic, durable participant harnesses designed for rapid guest turnaround. Starline Adventures supplies full-body adventure harnesses, sit/seat harnesses, chest harnesses, and youth-sized harnesses built specifically for continuous daily commercial operation across India.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Full-Body, Sit / Seat, Chest, and Dedicated Kids Harness models
  • High-tenacity 44mm / 45mm UV and abrasion-resistant polyester webbing
  • Minimum breaking strength rated to 22 kN (2200 kg) across load paths
  • Forged alloy steel dorsal & ventral attachment D-rings with anti-rust finish
  • Rapid self-locking adjustment buckles for high-throughput guest gearing
  • Compliant with CE / EN 361, EN 12277, and IS 3521 adventure & fall-arrest standards

Materials, Design & Engineering

Each harness utilizes abrasion-resistant 44mm/45mm high-tenacity polyester webbing paired with reinforced load-bearing stitch patterns and corrosion-protected forged alloy steel dorsal and ventral D-rings rated to a minimum breaking strength of 22 kN (2200 kg). Quick-adjust cam buckles and color-coded waist and leg loops enable park operators to fit visitors swiftly and verify proper strapping before launch.

Safety Compliance & Quality Inspection

Every harness is inspected for bar-tack stitch integrity, buckle locking action, and D-ring load compliance prior to dispatch. Clear color-differentiated straps help operators spot twists and confirm chest/waist tightness within seconds.

Before dispatch from our Nagpur facility, harness batches undergo visual stitch audits, hardware corrosion verification, webbing tension inspections, and traceable serial labeling for periodic park safety logs.

What types of adventure safety harnesses does Starline Adventures supply?
