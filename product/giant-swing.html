What is a Giant Swing?

A Giant Swing is a high-altitude adventure ride in which participants are secured in a harness system and released into a large swinging motion from an elevated structure. Giant Swings are commonly installed at adventure parks, resorts, tourism destinations and outdoor recreation projects.

Starline Adventures designs and manufactures Giant Swing systems according to project requirements, available site area, required height, rider capacity and installation conditions.

Giant Swing Manufacturing Process

Our manufacturing and project delivery process follows structured engineering stages to ensure structural integrity and smooth site deployment:

  1. Requirement & Site Assessment: Evaluating property boundaries, elevation, target throughput, and preferred tower height.
  2. Engineering & Design: Preparing structural drawings, load calculations, and foundation anchor specifications.
  3. Structural Fabrication: Precision fabrication of heavy mild steel A-frame structures and gusset plates.
  4. Mechanical & Rigging Assembly: Assembling electric brake motor winches, gearboxes, drums, and rigging connections.
  5. Factory Inspection/Testing: Component verification, weld inspections, and functional trials of winch and release components.
  6. Transportation & Site Installation: Safe transit of structural components followed by on-site erection and anchor mounting.
  7. Commissioning & Handover: Rigging installation, load testing, operator guidance, and project handover.

Frequently Asked Questions About Giant Swing

What is a Giant Swing?

A Giant Swing is an elevated pendulum thrill attraction where riders are secured in a harness system, winched to height, and released into a sweeping swinging arc over terrain.

What is the cost of a Giant Swing in India?

The cost of a Giant Swing in India depends on tower height, rider capacity, structural requirements, site terrain, foundation work, transportation, and installation. Starline Adventures provides customized project quotations based on your specific requirements.

What height options are available for a Giant Swing?

Typical tower height options include 40 ft, 50 ft, 60 ft, 70 ft, and up to 100 ft, depending on site conditions, topographical elevation, and project requirements.

How many people can ride a Giant Swing at one time?

Standard commercial installations accommodate 2 to 4 riders simultaneously, providing high throughput for commercial adventure parks and resorts.

Does Starline Adventures manufacture Giant Swings in India?

Yes, Starline Adventures manufactures Giant Swings in India, handling structural steel fabrication, mechanical assembly, equipment supply, and complete installation across all states.

Does Starline provide Giant Swing installation?

Yes, Starline Adventures provides turnkey installation and commissioning support across India, including foundation guidance, structural erection, rigging assembly, operational testing, and operator guidance.

Can the Giant Swing be customized according to the site?

Yes, Giant Swing systems can be customized in terms of tower height, seat capacity, foundation design, and site orientation to suit flat ground, hillside terrain, or cliff edges.

What information is required to get a Giant Swing quotation?

To receive an accurate quotation, provide your site location, available land dimensions, topography (flat, slope, or cliff), desired tower height, and preferred rider capacity (2 to 4 seater).

Where can Giant Swings be installed?

Giant Swings can be installed at adventure parks, theme parks, resorts, eco-tourism projects, hill stations, and commercial outdoor recreation centers with adequate spatial clearance.

What safety equipment is used in a Giant Swing?

Safety equipment includes multi-rider harness systems, high-strength alloy carabiners, primary and secondary safety attachments, galvanized wire ropes, mechanical quick-release locks, and electric winch emergency stop systems.
