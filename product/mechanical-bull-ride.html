Bull Ride Manufacturing & Installation

Starline Adventures manufactures high-grade bull ride using industrial structural steel (IS 2062), certified hardware, and anti-corrosive protective coatings. Every component is fabricated in our dedicated manufacturing facility under rigorous quality control standards to withstand extreme outdoor weather conditions and heavy commercial guest throughput.

Bull Ride Design & Engineering

Our engineering methodology for bull ride encompasses detailed structural calculations, load-bearing stress evaluations, and adherence to relevant national and international safety codes. We design custom mounting and foundational interfaces tailored to each client's specific site topography and operational requirements.

Bull Ride Safety Systems

Safety is engineered into every component of our bull ride. We incorporate redundant load paths, certified safety connectors, CE-compliant harness and cable interfaces, and multi-point proof load testing to ensure complete operational reliability.

Manufacturing Process
