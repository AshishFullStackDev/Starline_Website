What is Sky Cycling?

Sky Cycling, also known as Zip Bike or Sky Cycle, is an aerial adventure activity in which participants ride a specially configured bicycle along an elevated cable or track system. It is designed for adventure parks, resorts, tourism destinations and outdoor recreation projects.

Sky Cycling Manufacturing Process

Our manufacturing and project delivery process follows structured engineering steps to ensure precise fabrication and dependable site assembly:

  1. Requirement & Site Assessment: Evaluating property span, topographical clearance, terrain conditions, and target visitor flow.
  2. Track/Layout Planning: Designing single, double, or reciprocal line layouts according to site boundaries.
  3. Engineering & Design: Preparing structural terminal drawings, cable tensioning calculations, and foundation anchor specifications.
  4. Structural Fabrication: Precision cutting, welding, and anti-corrosive coating of mild steel terminal towers.
  5. Bicycle & Mechanical Component Modification: Adapting sports bicycle chassis with sealed bearings, stabilizer assemblies, and guide fixtures.
  6. Cable/Pulley System Assembly: Pre-assembling overhead safety pulleys, tandem wire guide wheels, and connection hardware.
  7. Factory Inspection & Testing: Verifying weld integrity, component alignment, and hardware fittings prior to dispatch.
  8. Transportation & Site Installation: Delivering structural components followed by on-site erection, cable stringing, and tensioning.
  9. Commissioning & Handover: Performing bicycle alignment, operational trial runs, operator training, and formal project handover.

Frequently Asked Questions About Sky Cycling

What is Sky Cycling?

Sky Cycling is an aerial adventure ride where participants pedal a specially adapted bicycle along high-tension cables suspended between structural towers or terminal platforms.

Is Sky Cycling also called Zip Bike or Sky Cycle?

Yes, Sky Cycling is commonly referred to as Zip Bike, Sky Cycle, or Aerial Cycling across commercial adventure parks and tourist destinations.

What is the cost of Sky Cycling in India?

The cost of Sky Cycling in India depends on track length, number of lanes and cycles, tower height, terrain, foundation requirements, and installation location. Starline Adventures provides customized project quotations based on your specific site conditions.

What track length is required for Sky Cycling?

Track length typically ranges from 50 meters to over 200 meters, customizable based on the available property span, elevation, and facility layout.

What is the maximum rider weight for Sky Cycling?

The maximum recommended rider weight is 110 kg, configured with safety harnesses and counterbalanced bicycle stabilizers.

What age group can use Sky Cycling?

Sky Cycling is generally suitable for participants aged 10 years and above who can comfortably reach the bicycle pedals and fit securely in the safety harness.

Does Starline Adventures manufacture Sky Cycling in India?

Yes, Starline Adventures manufactures Sky Cycling and Zip Bike systems in India, handling structural steel fabrication, bicycle modifications, equipment supply, and complete site installation.

Does Starline provide Sky Cycling installation?

Yes, Starline Adventures provides turnkey installation and commissioning support across all states in India, including foundation drawings, terminal tower erection, cable tensioning, and staff operational guidance.

Can Sky Cycling be customized according to the site?

Yes, Sky Cycling systems can be customized with single-lane, dual-lane, or reciprocal two-way configurations to suit natural gradients, resort landscapes, or adventure park layouts.

What information is required to get a Sky Cycling quotation?

To receive a tailored quotation, share your site location, available span length, terrain conditions (flat, slope, or water crossing), and desired lane configuration (single or dual cycle).

Can Sky Cycling be installed at resorts and adventure parks?

Yes, Sky Cycling is widely installed at commercial adventure parks, destination resorts, hill stations, eco-tourism projects, and agro-tourism centers with suitable span clearance.
