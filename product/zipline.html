What is a Zipline?

A zipline is an adventure activity in which a rider travels along a suspended steel wire rope using a trolley and safety harness system. Ziplines can be designed for different terrains, distances and rider capacities, including installations across hills, valleys, resorts and adventure parks.

Starline Adventures designs and manufactures customized zipline systems according to site conditions, span length, elevation difference, rider capacity and project requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a zipline cost in India?

The cost depends on zipline length, tower height, terrain, rider capacity, structural requirements, braking system, foundation, transportation and installation. Starline Adventures provides customized quotations based on project requirements.

What is the maximum length of a zipline?

Zipline length depends on the available site, elevation difference, terrain and engineering requirements. Starline Adventures can design customized systems for different project requirements.

Can a zipline be installed on flat land?

Yes. A zipline can be designed using suitable structural towers on flat terrain, subject to site evaluation and engineering requirements.

Does Starline Adventures provide zipline installation?

Yes. Starline Adventures provides zipline manufacturing, supply, installation and commissioning support across India.

Can you manufacture a customized zipline?

Yes. Zipline configuration, span length, tower arrangement, rider capacity and other project parameters can be customized according to site and project requirements.

How long does zipline installation take?

Installation time depends on span length, site conditions, foundation work, structural requirements and project scope. A project-specific timeline can be provided after reviewing the site and final design.

What braking systems are implemented on Starline Ziplines?

Depending on project configuration and speed profile, braking arrangements may include impact compression spring buffers, zip stopper deceleration systems, and backup arrest systems to provide controlled, safe landings.

What wire ropes are used for commercial zipline installations?

We utilize high-tensile galvanized steel wire ropes engineered for commercial cable tension, dynamic loads, and site-specific environmental conditions.

What terrain is required to install a commercial zipline?

Ziplines can be installed across flat ground with structural launch and landing towers, across natural hillside gradients, over valleys, or across water bodies.
