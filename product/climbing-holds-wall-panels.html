Climbing Holds & Wall Panels Manufacturer in India

Ergonomic micro-textured polyurethane climbing holds (jugs, slopers, pinches, crimps, macro volumes) and 3D FRP or 18mm–21mm birch multiplex panels with dense M10 T-nut grids.

Climbing Holds & Wall Panels Overview

The climb quality, route variety, and exterior longevity of any climbing wall depend on the surface panels and modular hold set. Starline Adventures manufactures 3D realistic FRP climbing panels, quartz-textured multiplex bouldering boards, and ergonomic polyurethane climbing holds engineered for indoor and outdoor commercial climbing arenas.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Complete grip sets: Jugs, Slopers, Pinches, Pockets, Crimps, Footholds & Volumes
  • 3D Realistic FRP Rock Panels & 18mm–21mm Birch Multiplex quartz-finish boards
  • Dense 100x100mm to 150x150mm stainless steel M10 T-nut mounting grid
  • Grade 8.8 / 304 stainless steel socket cap Allen bolts & top anchors
  • Micro-textured skin-friendly high-friction UV-stabilized resin finish
  • Complies with EN 12572-1 (Wall Structures) & EN 12572-3 (Holds)

Materials, Design & Engineering

Compliant with EN 12572-1 (wall structures) and EN 12572-3 (climbing holds), our panels feature precision-drilled 100mm × 100mm to 150mm × 150mm grids pre-fitted with 304 stainless steel M10 T-nuts. Holds are cast with dual-density reinforcement and stainless washers to resist spinning or cracking under heavy adult climbers.

Fasteners & Anchors Grade 8.8 / 304 stainless steel socket cap Allen bolts & dual-point top anchor stations

Safety Compliance & Quality Inspection

Smooth contoured hold edges reduce finger strain and skin abrasions for beginners, while steel insert rings inside bolt recesses prevent hold fracture when torqued to wall panels.

Every panel and hold batch undergoes thickness calibration, quartz surface adhesion testing, T-nut thread engagement verification, and resin color-fastness inspection before dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of climbing holds and wall panels does Starline supply?

We manufacture and supply modular polyurethane holds (jugs, slopers, pinches, pockets, crimps, footholds, and volumes) alongside 3D realistic FRP rock panels and 18mm–21mm quartz-coated birch multiplex boards.

What is the standard bolt and T-nut grid spacing on the panels?

Panels are CNC-drilled with 100mm × 100mm to 150mm × 150mm grid matrices fitted with stainless steel M10 T-nuts for Grade 8.8 / 304 stainless socket cap Allen bolts.

Can the holds and FRP panels withstand full outdoor sun and rain?

Yes. Both our 3D FRP rock panels and UV-stabilized polyurethane holds are formulated for exterior all-weather performance.

Do your holds and panels meet EN 12572 climbing wall standards?

Yes. Wall panel structures and climbing holds conform to EN 12572-1 and EN 12572-3 commercial requirements.

Can I choose custom colors or kid-friendly routes for my park?

Yes. We supply color-coded route packs tailored to beginners, kids zones, or technical climbing routes.
