What is 4 in 1 Bungee Jumping?

4 in 1 Bungee Jumping is a multi-station adventure attraction that allows four participants to use individual bungee systems and trampolines simultaneously. The setup is designed for commercial recreation spaces such as adventure parks, resorts, family entertainment areas and tourism destinations.

4 in 1 Bungee Jumping Manufacturing Process

Our manufacturing and project delivery process follows structured engineering steps to ensure build precision and smooth installation:

  1. Requirement & Site Assessment: Reviewing venue footprint, ceiling height (for indoor setups), and guest traffic expectations.
  2. Engineering & Layout Design: Generating layout drawings, structural stress assessments, and interface details.
  3. Structural Fabrication: Precision cutting, drilling, and welding of structural steel framework and mounting points.
  4. Mechanical Component Integration: Integrating electric brake winches, worm gearboxes, control panels, and pulleys.
  5. Bungee & Trampoline System Assembly: Pre-fitting high-elasticity bungee assemblies, commercial trampolines, and safety padding.
  6. Factory Inspection & Testing: Verifying winch mechanisms, electrical control circuits, and structural connections.
  7. Transportation & Site Installation: Safe transit of equipment followed by on-site structural assembly and positioning.
  8. Commissioning & Handover: Conducting operational trials, operator guidance, and project handover.

Frequently Asked Questions About 4 in 1 Bungee Jumping

What is 4 in 1 Bungee Jumping?

4 in 1 Bungee Jumping is a multi-station adventure attraction that allows four participants to bounce and perform aerial maneuvers on individual trampolines connected to motorized bungee suspension systems simultaneously.

What is the price of 4 in 1 Bungee Jumping in India?

The price of a 4 in 1 Bungee Jumping setup in India depends on the structural specifications, footprint, motor and winch configurations, site requirements, and installation location. Starline Adventures offers customized project-specific pricing based on your venue requirements.

How many people can use the system at one time?

A standard 4 in 1 Bungee Jumping setup accommodates 4 participants simultaneously across four independent trampoline stations.

What is the required installation area?

The standard installation footprint requires an area of approximately 35 ft x 35 ft with adequate vertical clearance for jumpers. Custom dimensions can be configured for specific indoor or outdoor layouts.

What is the maximum participant weight?

The standard maximum participant weight is 80 kg per jumper. Operating limits can be adjusted based on the bungee cord assembly and engineering design.

Does Starline provide installation across India?
