Glass Bridge Manufacturer in India

Spectacular suspended glass walkway skywalks engineered with multi-layer toughened laminated glass and heavy structural steel trusses for tourism landmarks.

Glass Bridge Manufacturing & Installation

Constructed with heavy-gauge structural steel trusses, multi-layer toughened laminated safety glass sandwich panels (SentryGlas interlayer), high-tensile stay wire ropes, and stainless steel marine-grade balustrades.

Glass Bridge Design & Engineering

Structural design conforms to high wind-load simulations, seismic parameters, and heavy crowd live-load calculations (up to 500 kg/m²). Multiple redundant load paths ensure total stability.

Technical Specifications

Detailed mechanical, structural, and operational parameters for Glass Bridge as manufactured by Starline Adventures:

Age Group All ages (5 years & above)
Installation Type Outdoor Valley / Hill / Resort
Guest Capacity 50 to 100+ visitors at once
Bridge Span 20m to 150m+ (Site tailored)
Materials & Components High-grade structural steel trusses, toughened laminated multi-layer sandwich glass panels, galvanised stay wire ropes, turnbuckles, stainless steel safety balustrades and handrails.

Glass Bridge Safety Systems

Triple-layer laminated glass panels engineered such that even in the unlikely event of top glass damage, the structural interlayers maintain full load-bearing capacity without failure. Includes anti-slip surface treatments.

Manufacturing Process

Full FEA stress analysis, precision steel truss fabrication, ultrasonic weld inspection, glass batch destructive load testing, and site anchor civil construction.

Installation & Commissioning

Complete turnkey installation including hillside/rock anchor drilling, truss assembly, glass panel precision placement with silicone weather sealing, and proof load verification.
