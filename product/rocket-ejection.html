What is a Rocket Ejection Ride?

A Rocket Ejection Ride is a high-thrill adventure attraction that uses a controlled launch system to propel riders vertically upward and provide an intense acceleration and descent experience. It is designed for suitable adventure parks, resorts, tourism destinations and commercial recreation projects.

Starline Adventures provides customized Rocket Ejection Ride solutions based on site conditions, tower configuration, rider capacity, structural requirements and project specifications.

Rocket Ejection Manufacturing & Installation

Starline Adventures manufactures commercial Rocket Ejection Ride systems using structural steel towers, mechanical launch components, winch systems, bungee systems, control equipment and rider safety equipment.

Each installation can be customized according to the available site area, tower configuration, rider capacity, project requirements and final engineering design.

Rocket Ejection Ride Safety Systems

Safety is an important part of every commercial Rocket Ejection Ride installation. Depending on the ride configuration, safety provisions may include rider harnesses, helmets, secure attachment systems, emergency power shutoff, mechanical safety systems, cable arrest arrangements and controlled spectator areas.

The final safety system is selected according to the ride design, site conditions, rider capacity and applicable project requirements.

What is a Rocket Ejection Ride?

How much does a Rocket Ejection Ride cost in India?

How high does a Rocket Ejection Ride go?

The launch height depends on the tower configuration, ride design and project requirements. The final operating parameters are determined during engineering and design.

How high does the Rocket Ejection propel riders?

Depending on tower height, bungee cord configuration, and tension settings, riders are catapulted 30ft to 40ft+ vertically skyward.

What is the recommended age for a Rocket Ejection Ride?

The current standard specification on this page recommends riders aged 10 years and above, with a maximum rider weight of 100 kg. Final participant restrictions should follow the approved ride design and operating requirements.
